Victor Shammas is Associate Professor of Sociology at the University of Bergen and holds a PhD from the University of Oslo (2017). His work moves across political economy, critical theory, comparative penology, and digital sociology, with articles published in journals such as _Punishment & Society_, _British Journal of Criminology_, _Constellations_, and _Capital & Class_. He also writes the newsletter [_Theory Brief_](theorybrief.com) — about global politics, culture, and social theory. Research: [victorshammas.com](https://victorshammas.com)
