Network Analyzer is a tool under development for mapping social networks as sociologists and anthropologists actually encounter them: as people affiliated with venues — editorial boards, think tanks, committees, conferences, meetings. Messy source material (a pasted masthead, a committee roster, a spreadsheet of appointments) is turned into structured relational data through AI-assisted entry and clean-up, and the resulting network is explored through interactive visualization: bipartite people–venue graphs, co-membership projections, community detection, and centrality rankings that surface the brokers and cross-members who hold a field together.
